# Prompt 03 — Capa visual

```text
Create a photorealistic cinematic editorial cover image for a fictional multimedia story titled conceptually 'The Last Message'. A young Brazilian woman in her late twenties, short dark curly hair, olive skin, wearing a faded mustard jacket, sits alone at a small kitchen table at dawn, looking at an old smartphone with a cracked screen. A red ceramic mug, a handwritten folded letter, and a tiny potted plant are on the table. Soft blue morning light enters through a rainy window, warm tungsten light from the kitchen creates realistic mixed lighting, intimate documentary photography, subtle film grain, natural skin texture, imperfect details, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens, restrained color grading, no visible logos, no text, no watermark. Composition: 16:9 landscape, subject on the right third, generous darker negative space on the left for README/social copy, believable human moment, quiet tension, not surreal, not glossy, not AI-looking.
```

- **Formato:** 16:9
- **Saída:** PNG
- **Fundo transparente:** não
- **Texto na imagem:** não
- **Uso:** capa do README e peça de divulgação
